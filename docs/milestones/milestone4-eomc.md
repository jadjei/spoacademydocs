| Item | Link |

## F. Beta Test Cohort Results and Iteration

**Acceptance criteria:** Feedback is collected from participants as appropriate. Actionable
improvements are identified based on feedback.

**Evidence required:** Compiled feedback report from participants. Document outlining
identified improvements and action plan.

**Evidence**

- [Compiled feedback report](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/feedback-report): what participants said, by theme, with consented quotes and the channel each item came from.
- [Improvements and action plan](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/improvements-and-actions): each improvement traced to the feedback that prompted it, with an owner and a date, and the platform items raised with Andamio kept separate.

The written instrument is a seven-question form, all questions optional, with a three-way
quoting-consent item. Issued in the session 3 call on 19 October, in the cohort Discord, and by
email to the cohort list on 24 October. Responses are held privately; nothing is attributed
without the respondent's recorded consent.

Feedback raised by participants during the run, and the action taken:

| Raised by participants | Action taken |
|---|---|
| Minting the access token took longer than planned; wallet connection and collateral caused difficulty | Worked through live on the call; help offered in the cohort channel; token cost refunded |
| Session 2 slot clashed with participant travel | Date moved by participant poll |
| Module numbers appearing inline confuse participants before the Andamio structure is explained | Pulled back to the module overview table only |

Source: [onboarding write-up](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/2026-09-21-onboarding),
[session 1 write-up](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/2026-09-28-session1),
[session 2 write-up](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/2026-10-12-session2),
[session 3 write-up](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/2026-10-19-session3).

The form closed after the final session. Three of nine enrolled participants responded in
writing, all three consenting to be quoted by name and pool ticker. The final session played the
findings back to the cohort on the recorded call and then went round the room for what the form
had missed, which surfaced four further themes including the cohort's strongest product ask, a
credentials package an operator can show to partners and funders. That round is recorded in the
[session 4 write-up](https://catalyst.pages.fluid7.co.uk/spoacademydocs/docs/cohort/2026-10-26-session4).

The action plan carries seventeen improvements we own, each with an owner and a date, six
platform items raised with Andamio, and four already closed. Both documents are final as of
27 October 2026.
