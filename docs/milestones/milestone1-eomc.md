1. SPO Business Persona Definition:
 - A document containing detailed descriptions of at least 3 distinct SPO business personas. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
2. Learning Objectives Clarity:
 - The course design documents include sections on the 5 key areas (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
3. Business-Focused Course Structure:
 - A detailed course outline document or syllabus. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
 - The outline includes modules on SPO business operations, unique selling points, and bootstrap opportunities. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
 - A visual representation (e.g., flowchart) showing the progression of topics from startup to growth stages. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
4. PBL Design for Business Challenges:
 - A document describing at least 3 hypothetical SPO business scenarios or challenges. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
 - For each scenario, a breakdown of the problem, learning objectives, and expected outcomes. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
5. UI/UX Design:
 - [Design mockups (Miro, Figma, Inkscape) for at least 3 key course interface pages.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZbPG95xRnqzNvyiArntcD5-FFexNu7WM/view?usp=sharing)
 - [Brief explanation notes for each mockup, highlighting key features and design decisions.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZbPG95xRnqzNvyiArntcD5-FFexNu7WM/view?usp=sharing)
6. Site Structure Usability:
 - [A sitemap document showing the overall structure of the course platform.](https://app.andamio.io/course/spo-academy)
 - A user flow diagram illustrating how users will navigate through the course. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
 - Minutes or notes from relevant project team meetings as appropriate. (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
