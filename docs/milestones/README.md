# Project Catalyst Milestones

This section tracks the deliverables and progress for the SPO Academy Project Catalyst funding milestones.

## Milestone Overview

### Milestone 1: Course Foundation and User Experience Design
- **Status**: Completed — approved by two reviewers, August 2024
- **Focus**: Business persona definition, learning objectives, course structure, UI/UX design
- **Key Deliverables**: SPO business personas, learning objectives framework, course outline, PBL scenarios, design mockups, site structure

### Milestone 2: Technical Infrastructure and Content Development
- **Status**: Completed — signed off September 2025
- **Focus**: Treasury smart contract, Andamio blockchain functionality, course content, video pre-production, front-end components
- **Key Deliverables**: Treasury smart contract, slide decks for all modules, video scripts and storyboards, front-end implementation

### Milestone 3: Platform Enhancement and Course Content Finalization
- **Status**: In progress
- **Focus**: Completed blockchain functionality, refined UI, assessment tools, finished video content, payment processing, interactive course elements
- **Progress at 2026-07-30**:
  - Course content built out on the platform — 8 modules, 39 learning targets, 38 lessons, 8 introductions, 172 figures
  - Assessment tools delivered — one assignment per module, evidencing all 39 learning targets
  - Outstanding: video content, payment processing, and confirmation of the blockchain, front-end and interactive-component work

### Milestone 4 (Final): Platform Launch, Community Engagement, and Project Evaluation
- **Status**: In progress — cohort running, completes 26 October 2026
- **Focus**: Treasury seeding, live test cohort, pricing model, marketing campaign, on-chain assets, cohort feedback, close-out report and video
- **Progress at 2026-10-02**: marketing campaign complete across every named channel; six participants confirmed and the cohort two sessions in; credential mechanism live with assessments accepted

## Key Achievements

### ✅ Completed Deliverables

**Design and definition (Milestone 1)**
- SPO business personas across customer and channel avatar groups
- Course structure spanning startup to growth stages, with measurable learning objectives
- PBL scenarios for SPO business challenges
- UI/UX design mockups and site structure diagrams

**Build and content (Milestones 2-3)**
- Slide decks for all modules, plus video scripts and storyboards
- Treasury smart contract and front-end components
- Front-end website implementation at [spo.academy](https://spo.academy)
- 8 modules live on Andamio (000-700), every learning target carrying a lesson
- One assignment per module, covering all 39 learning targets
- 172 diagrams and figures served from the platform CDN
