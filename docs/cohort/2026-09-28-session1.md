## What was covered

**Modules 000 and 100**, alongside a live clinic on minting the Andamio access token.

The token step took longer than planned, and that is worth saying plainly rather than
glossing over: wallet connection and collateral caught people out. Collateral was worked
through on the call. If you are still stuck, ask in the cohort Discord and we will finish it
with you rather than leave you to it.

Harry also previewed the **Essence framework**, which sorts an organisation into four
areas: your essence, your internal workings, your market environment, and the wider forces
acting on all of it. That structure carries the rest of the course.

## What was discussed

- Reaching delegators, and how hard attention is to win even when you already have an
  engaged community around the pool.
- Why a pool can be technically excellent and still stay commercially invisible, and why
  more technical work does not close that gap.
- How business models built for other industries have to be adapted for blockchain, rather
  than adopted unchanged.
- The chain parameters target 500 pools. That is what the protocol aims at, not a count of
  pools already operating at optimal size.

Points raised in the sessions are recorded here; nothing is attributed to a named
participant.

## What participants need to do

1. **Work Your Journey Toolkit** and complete its assignment. Fifteen to
   thirty minutes.
2. **Start Your Why** if you have the time.
3. **Submit assignments in Andamio** as you finish them, so they can be reviewed and your
   credential issued.
4. **Still not minted the access token?** Say so in the cohort Discord. The cost is refunded.
