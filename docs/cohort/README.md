The Fund 12 cohort runs as four live sessions on consecutive Mondays, alongside the
self-paced material on Andamio. This section is the record of each session: what was
covered, what participants need to do next, and a recording where one exists.

It is written for two readers. Cohort members who missed a session and want to catch up,
and anyone reviewing how the cohort was delivered.
