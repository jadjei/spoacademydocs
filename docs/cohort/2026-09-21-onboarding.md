## What the Academy is

A Project Catalyst funded course on the **business and marketing side** of running a stake
pool, deliberately not the technical side. Technical questions are still answered when they
come up, they are simply not the subject.

The premise put to the group: a pool can be technically excellent, hit every block and keep
uptime clean, and still not grow. That gap is not a technical problem, so technical work does
not close it.

## How the course runs

- **Eight modules** on the [Andamio](https://app.andamio.io) platform, worked at your own pace.
- **Four live sessions**, on consecutive Mondays at 16:00 UTC.
- Roughly **two to three hours a week** for about a month.
- Each module ends in a short assignment, 15 to 30 minutes. The assignments accumulate into a
  business plan you keep.
- An **on-chain credential** is issued on completion, held in your own wallet.

The live sessions are the forcing function rather than the content. The material stands on its
own and is available whenever it suits you.

See the [module reference](../course/modules.md) for the full structure.

## What was discussed

The session covered the state of delegation in the current market and why growth is hard for
smaller pools. Themes raised by the group:

- The size of the stake that has **never been delegated to anything**, and whether pool growth
  is better understood as activating non-delegators than as competing for stake already placed.
- That different ecosystem projects need **different value propositions** when a pool talks to
  prospective delegators, rather than one general pitch.
- How visibility of staking rewards, or the lack of it, affects whether delegators stay engaged.
- The scale of the competitive field, and that the chain parameters target 500 pools rather than
  that number already operating at optimal size.

## What participants need to do

1. **Have a mainnet Cardano wallet ready.** A fresh, separate wallet is fine. This is where
   your credential is issued, so it needs to be one you control.
2. **Mint the Andamio access token** when the link is sent. This costs a small amount of ADA
   on mainnet, and **Fluid7 refunds that cost**.
3. **Work the modules asynchronously**, allowing two to three hours a week.
