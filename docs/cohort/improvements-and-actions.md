## How this was closed with the cohort

The final session on 26 October played this back: what the cohort said, and what changes
because of it, in front of the people who said it, and the call then went round the room for
anything the form had missed. That is recorded in the
[session 4 write-up](./2026-10-26-session4.md). One further form response arrived within the
hour after the session and is included above.

The course remains open and self-paced, so feedback keeps arriving after the cohort has
formally ended. Anything new is added here with an owner and a date in the same way.
