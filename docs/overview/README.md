# Project Overview

## Vision Statement

SPO Academy empowers Stake Pool Operators on the Cardano blockchain to transform their technical expertise into sustainable, thriving business operations through comprehensive project-based learning.

## Mission

To bridge the gap between technical proficiency and business acumen in the Cardano ecosystem by providing SPOs with practical frameworks, real-world scenarios, and blockchain-verified credentials.

## Target Audience

### Primary SPO Personas

The SPO Business Persona Document (available to Catalyst reviewers on request)
delivered in Milestone 1 defines two avatar groups: the **customers** who run pools, and
the **channels** that reach them.

**Customers — individuals and organisations running a stake pool on Cardano.** They are
segmented by why they run it, because that motivation shapes what a viable pool means to
them:

| Segment | Motivation | Example avatars |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning-led** | Building the node and a delegator engagement business as a way to learn | Techy Adopters |
| **External focus** | Using the pool to build community and engagement beyond it | Jonathan (investment-focused), Fletcher (saver-to-investor platform), CNC (impact-led community of interest) |
| **Internal focus** | Pool income funding an organisation's activity in the Catalyst ecosystem | Amir (regional adoption), SWARM, Sanada |

**Channels — SPO organisations and associations**, who reach their members on our behalf
and may become customers themselves, whether white-labelling the programme or taking
coaching on SPO growth. Examples: XSPO, CNC, C4C, and regional bodies.

**Writing for the aggregate.** Course and assignment content addresses the common shape
rather than any one avatar: an operator, solo or with a small team, whose gap is business
skill rather than technical skill, and for whom the pool is often a means to something
else. Hence content speaks of "your pool and whatever it exists to support".

## Key Objectives

- **Business Skills Development**: Transform technical operators into business-savvy entrepreneurs
- **Sustainable Operations**: Teach frameworks for long-term viability and growth
- **Community Building**: Foster connections between SPOs, mentors, and industry experts
- **Blockchain Credentials**: Provide verifiable, on-chain certification of completion

## Success Metrics

- Course completion rates
- SPO business performance improvements
- Community engagement levels
- Credential verification and recognition
- Long-term pool sustainability
